    Roadmap to the platinum

    Five stages, in order, built from the official trophy descriptions. The story comes first because the game has no mission replay — keep a manual save before every story mission. Everything after the story can be done in free roam.

      Missable trophies — 13 to protect

      A trophy is flagged missable here when its official description ties it to a story chapter or mission in a game with no mission replay, or when published guides flag its moment as one-time.
