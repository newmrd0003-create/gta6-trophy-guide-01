        How this guide is built

        Every trophy name, description and grade is the official PlayStation 5 list as published before this guide was written: names, descriptions and grades from the official list on Exophase, PSN progress targets from PSNProfiles. The two agreed on every name, description and grade: 55 trophies — 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze.

        Every guide on this page is written in our own words from the official description alone. The game is a day old, so no guide step here has been checked in the game yet. Where a place or a step is not pinned down by the official description, the guide says so instead of inventing it.
