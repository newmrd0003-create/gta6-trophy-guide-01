Roadmap to the Platinum

Five stages, in order. Story first — the missable trophies all live in Stages 1 and 2 — then strands, activities, the big map sweep, and a final clean-up. Each stage lists the trophies it earns; tap one to open its guide in the tracker.

Missable trophies — do these on time

A trophy is flagged missable when its official description ties it to one named mission, one character's final mission, a moment with your partner, or a choice. The game had not launched when this guide was written, so treat every flag as our reading of the official description — and replay a named mission promptly if its trophy does not pop.
